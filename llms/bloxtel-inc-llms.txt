# Bloxtel, Inc.

> Bloxtel builds a decentralized private 5G platform that lets enterprises stand up standalone 5G networks in hours. Its all-in-one 5G Access Point runs Open6GC (Bloxtel's decentralized network core) and pairs with tokenized dSIM electronic-SIM technology and a Web3 Operator dApp for network configuration, subscription management, and AI-powered predictive maintenance.
